Focused Checker LLM Testing Cases
Model: Kaggle + DDXPlus XGBoost
Purpose: Patient-style LLM chat testing using symptom wording adapted from reliable public medical websites.

Method note:
- Each input was written in first-person form (for example, "I have ...").
- The symptom wording was kept as close as possible to the source website phrasing.
- No new clinical symptom terms were intentionally introduced beyond simple sentence connectors.
- Results below were tested against the focused checker in the prototype.

CASE 01
Target disease: acute bronchitis
Source URL:
https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/bronchitis/symptoms-causes/syc-20355566
Source symptom wording used:
- Cough
- Production of mucus (sputum), which can be clear, white, yellowish-gray or green in color
- Sore throat
- Slight fever and chills
- Shortness of breath and wheezing
- Chest discomfort
Patient-style input:
I have a cough, often with clear, white, yellow or green mucus, wheezing or a whistling sound when breathing, shortness of breath, especially with activity, sore throat, often before the cough begins, and a low-grade fever.
Extracted symptoms:
- cough
- coughing up sputum
- fever
- shortness of breath
- sore throat
- wheezing
Top-3 result:
1. acute bronchitis (99.69%)
2. croup (0.14%)
3. pneumonia (0.03%)
Status: Top-1 matched
